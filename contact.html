Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery usually take? ▼
Most orders in Greater Noida are delivered within 25 to 30 minutes of placing the order. You can track your rider in real time!
How do I apply coupon codes? ▼
Navigate to your Cart page, enter any active promo code (like WELCOME50) into the Promo Code box, and click Apply.
What payment methods are supported? ▼
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, and Net Banking.
What is the cancellation and refund policy? ▼
You can cancel an order within 60 seconds of placing it. Any prepaid amount will be refunded to your original payment mode instantly.
